Twelve tabs

Each one does a single job well enough that you don't have to leave. The preset bar and the output meter stay where they are on every tab.

Load, chop and play. REX loops can spread their slices across empty pads or stay whole on one pad. Export chops as a REX loop, or save the loop WAV, individual slices and groove MIDI for your DAW.

The whole beat in one grid. Accent, ratchet, flam and nudge on any step, and per-step changes to pan, cutoff and start. There are generators too, for when you're stuck.

Split a loop into frequency bands, or into its drums and everything else. Solo or mute each part, then put one on a pad or drag it into your DAW.

Two decks and a crossfader. Scratch something, then Deck Chop puts the cut on your pads. Load a REX loop to turn its slices into chops on empty pads, or export your deck chops as REX or WAV with slices and MIDI. No jog wheel? Scratch on a knob, the mod wheel or the pitch wheel.

A piano roll for the synth, up to 32 bars, and a melody for any pad, with chords, ghost notes and a CC lane. MIDI in and out.

Up to four synth tracks, each with its own sound and mixer strip. Choose Synth, Samples, Electric Piano, Organ, String Machine, FM Keys, Plucked Strings, Electric Bass, Upright Bass, Bell and Mallet or Granular. The synth still has three oscillators, wavetables, modulation, an arpeggiator and over 550 presets. The new voices share the SYNTH mixer strip, save their settings with your project and render through SYNTH-part WAV export.

Shape Electric Piano with Tone, Decay and tremolo, Organ with nine drawbars and rotary, or String Machine with its envelope, tone and stereo ensemble. These three voices are built in; no sample files are needed. Keyboard instrument guide.

Samples plays supported SFZ and DSPreset instruments. Room Upright piano and Porch Bass upright bass are optional local library packages, installed separately; their samples are not in the plugin installer. You can also load your own supported presets. Sample library guide.

One bass line that slides. An 808, another bass sound, or your own sample, with a slide or an accent on any note.

Chain the four banks into a song, or let it build one from your patterns, with fills and drop-outs before each hook. Give a section a Telephone, Lo-fi or Hall treatment and hear it change.

Drop a vocal or a sample onto the audio lanes, or record a mic straight into the song. Launch patterns and loops on the bar and keep the take as sections. Select bars across every lane to cut, copy or duplicate, bounce a section to a pad, open a MIDI file as a song, and export a music video with a visualiser.

A strip for every sound, four buses and a master rack. The master has LUFS, true peak and a correlation meter, so you can tell when it's loud enough.

Cue any strip in your headphones, put a pad or the bass up to half a second ahead of the beat or behind it, load your own impulse responses into the Convolution Reverb, and switch any insert on and off from a controller button or the song.

Four macros that each move up to eight controls, and four XY pads: two for the macros, a filter and a beat repeat. Put them on your controller and play the mix.

Four tape loops that play beside your pads, locked to the beat like a tape machine. Half or double speed, reverse, and a Wear knob for that worn-tape wobble and hiss. They're in your exports, with their own strip on the mixer.

Record a loop straight in, in time with the song, then stack layers on it like a looper pedal: switch each one in and out from your controller, turn it down, transpose or reverse it, and flatten them when you're done.

Dig through your own crates: every file analysed for tempo, key and sound, filtered, rated and dragged straight into your DAW. And a reference crate to listen to. Nothing gets downloaded or ripped.

Put your controller to work with MIDI Learn for the plugin's automatable controls. The new keyboard instruments' sound controls save with your project; they don't yet have host automation.

One plugin

Pads used to be one of three. The other two, Producer Decks and Key Sampler, now live inside it, so there's one thing to install and one place to make the whole beat.

  • Turntables Two decks, scratching, Deck Chop and a record's stems. Was Producer Decks. In Pads
  • Producer Pads Sampling, sequencing, arranging, mixing. This page. Out now
  • Key zones A sample played up and down a keyboard, on any pad. Was Key Sampler. In Pads

Why I made it

I was already making beats, and the pads were already on the desk. What kept getting in the way was everything in between. Menus I didn't need, a workflow I had to bend myself around, and pricing that quietly assumed I lived somewhere else. Writing my own stopped looking ridiculous somewhere around the third time I gave up on someone else's.

What I run is an MPD18, an Oxygen 25 and a Keystation 49es. All controllers, and they need something to play. So I built that part, and it still gets tested on those three.

It's made in Southern Africa, by one person. If something breaks, tell me and I'll fix it.

Questions people ask

Is $0 really OK?

Yes. Type 0 and you get the same installer as everyone else. Use it on anything, commercial included. If it ends up on a record and you feel like paying later, come back.

Which DAWs does it work in?

I test in FL Studio, Ableton Live and Reason. Any VST3 host should be fine, and the CLAP version is there for Bitwig. Logic needs a Mac build, which isn't done yet.

Do I need a pad controller?

No. Click the pads or use your computer keyboard. If you do have a controller, everything can be MIDI-learned.

Does it come with sounds?

Yes: the demo drum kit, over 550 synth presets, and built-in Electric Piano, Organ and String Machine voices. Bring your own samples and breaks, or load supported sampled-instrument presets. Room Upright and Porch Bass are optional local packages; their sample banks are not bundled in the installer.

Can I move chopped loops into my DAW?

Export pad or deck chops as a REX loop, or export a whole-loop WAV, individual slice WAVs and groove MIDI. REX files can also load as slices or stay whole on a pad. External Reason/ReCycle file compatibility and listening checks are still pending; use WAV and MIDI if you need that alternative.

I found a bug.

Good, honestly. Open an issue on GitHub with what you did and what happened, and which DAW you were in.

How do I update?

Download the new installer and run it over the old one. Every release has notes on GitHub saying what changed.
