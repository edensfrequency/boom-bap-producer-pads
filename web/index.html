The trailer

A minute, recorded straight out of the plugin with nothing else running. I cut it from eleven one-minute videos, one feature each.

Eleven tabs

Each one does a single job well enough that you don't have to leave. The preset bar and the output meter stay where they are on every tab.

Load, chop and play. The browser, the pads, the sample editor and the sound of each pad, all on one screen.

The whole beat in one grid. Accent, ratchet, flam and nudge on any step, and per-step changes to pan, cutoff and start. There are generators too, for when you're stuck.

Split a loop into frequency bands, or into its drums and everything else. Solo or mute each part, then put one on a pad or drag it into your DAW.

Two decks and a crossfader. Scratch something, then Deck Chop puts the cut on your pads. If scratching is the whole point, Producer Decks goes further.

A piano roll for the synth, 1 to 8 bars, and a melody for any pad. MIDI in and out.

Three oscillators with wavetables, a filter, envelopes, LFOs, a mod matrix, an arpeggiator and chords. 30 presets to start from.

One bass line that slides. An 808, another bass sound, or your own sample, with a slide or an accent on any note.

Chain the four banks into a song: intro, verse, hook, whatever you call them, each with its own length.

A strip for every sound, two buses and a master rack. The master has LUFS, true peak and a correlation meter, so you can tell when it's loud enough.

Four macros that each move up to eight controls, and an XY pad. Put them on your controller and play the mix.

Dig through your own crates, and a reference crate to listen to. Nothing gets downloaded or ripped.

Every knob and switch takes MIDI Learn. Not a handful I picked for you. All of them, so whatever controller you own can play it.

Why I made it

I was already making beats, and the pads were already on the desk. What kept getting in the way was everything in between. Menus I didn't need, a workflow I had to bend myself around, and pricing that quietly assumed I lived somewhere else. Writing my own stopped looking ridiculous somewhere around the third time I gave up on someone else's.

What I run is an MPD18, an Oxygen 25 and a Keystation 49es. All controllers, and they need something to play. So I built that part, and it still gets tested on those three.

It's made in Southern Africa, by one person. If something breaks, tell me and I'll fix it.

Questions people ask

Is $0 really OK?

Yes. Type 0 and you get the same installer as everyone else. Use it on anything, commercial included. If it ends up on a record and you feel like paying later, come back.

Which DAWs does it work in?

I test in FL Studio, Ableton Live and Reason. Any VST3 host should be fine, and the CLAP version is there for Bitwig. Logic needs a Mac build, which isn't done yet.

Do I need a pad controller?

No. Click the pads or use your computer keyboard. If you do have a controller, everything can be MIDI-learned.

Does it come with sounds?

The demo kit you can play at the top of this page. Past that, bring your own samples and breaks.

I found a bug.

Good, honestly. Open an issue on GitHub with what you did and what happened, and which DAW you were in.

How do I update?

Download the new installer and run it over the old one. Every release has notes on GitHub saying what changed.
