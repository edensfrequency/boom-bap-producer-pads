# Electric Piano

Choose **SYNTH > Instrument > Electric Piano**. This is a built-in modelled
voice, so no sample file or library download is needed. Play MIDI channel 5,
the keyboard below or the SYNTH part. Its range is A0-C8 (MIDI notes 21-108).

- **Tone** changes the brightness of new strikes.
- **Decay** changes how long newly struck notes ring naturally.
- **Tremolo** changes modulation depth as you play.
- **Rate** sets tremolo speed from 0.1 to 12 Hz.

Tone and Decay take effect on the next note. Tremolo controls also affect
held notes. The sustain pedal holds released keys; pitch wheel range is
+/-2 semitones. The model uses A4=440 Hz. Synth MPE, tuning/glide and modulation
settings do not apply to this voice.

The **SYNTH mixer strip** controls level, pan, mute/solo, sends and strip
processing. Master pitch also applies. The **SYNTH part's WAV export** uses
the selected EP with a separate voice engine, so exporting does not disturb
live notes.

Source choice and all four settings are saved with your project. Switch among
Synth, Samples and Electric Piano from Instrument; each switch clears held
notes. Choosing a Synth preset selects Synth again. Existing sample-preset
references and the installed Room Upright/Porch Bass library stay in place.

The four EP controls are project settings; they are not exposed as new DAW
automation parameters or included in Synth sound presets/A-B. Synth-only sound
exports and editing controls are hidden for this source. Use SYNTH-part WAV
export to render a phrase.

This is an original velocity-sensitive modal sound design with 32 voices,
not sampled factory content or a calibrated reproduction of named hardware.
