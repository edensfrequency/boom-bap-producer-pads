# Porch Bass

With the optional local content installed, choose **SYNTH > Instrument >
Samples > Library... > Porch Bass**. Play **C1-A3 (MIDI notes 24-57)** from
MIDI channel 5, the SYNTH keyboard or the SYNTH part. Notes outside that range
are silent. The SYNTH mixer strip controls volume, pan, mute/solo and sends.
The selected instrument also feeds the SYNTH part's WAV export.

Porch Bass is a recorded pizzicato upright-bass voice: soft/loud layers switch
between velocities 80 and 81, with two alternating takes per layer. It needs
about 39.27 MiB decoded. The user accepted its rendered audition on 2026-11-09.
That listening result does not mean every note and playing technique was
individually auditioned.

The project-authored limited mapping uses CC0-1.0 recordings by D. Smolken from
[this pinned source](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/dsmolken.double-bass/tree/c2985eb647109d2a8f30a70071e3e163339d7396).
It covers the plucked voice with a short release, not the source's complete
articulations, noise keys or controls. Original recordings are unchanged;
licence, source map, credits and provenance accompany the content.

Installed content lives under `%ProgramData%\Boom Bap Producer Pads\Instruments`.
Keep `programs\porch-bass-v1` in place: projects store its absolute preset path.
Room Upright and existing pilot-project paths remain in place. The current
release installer does not include this optional content package yet.
