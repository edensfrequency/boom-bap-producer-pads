# SYNTH tab: a synthesiser to play

**In one line:** a synth you play from the keyboard, your controller or your DAW -- keys, pads, leads and basses next to your samples.

## What this part of the plugin is for

The pads play samples. The SYNTH tab makes its own sound: three
oscillators through a filter, shaped by envelopes and moved by LFOs. Pick a
preset (Warm Keys, Soft Pad, Saw Lead, 808, Reese, Acid...) and play, or
build a sound from Init. The tab is an instrument slot: the synth is the
synthesis source in it. Choose Samples to load a supported SFZ or DecentSampler
preset alongside it; see the guide below.

## Questions this answers

- *How do I play it?* Open the tab and use the keyboard at the bottom, or send MIDI on channel 5 from your DAW or controller.
- *Can I make a wavetable from my own sample?* Yes: set an oscillator to Wavetable and click **Table...**, or drop an audio file on the oscillator.
- *Where do my presets go?* Documents / Boom Bap Producer Pads / Synth Presets, as .bbsynth files.
- *Is the sound saved with my project?* Yes, the whole sound.
