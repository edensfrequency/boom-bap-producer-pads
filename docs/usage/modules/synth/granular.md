# Granular

In **SYNTH > Instrument**, choose **Granular**. Each note you play is a cloud
of short, overlapping grains -- the same grain engine as a pad's Granular
mode -- read from a built-in sound and played at the note's pitch. The sound
is a voice-like tone whose vowel changes slowly from one end to the other,
so where the grains play and how fast they move change the colour. No
samples or downloads are needed. Play the keyboard, MIDI channel 5, or the
SYNTH part.

The sound menu beside Instrument applies one of three starting sounds:
**Slow Cloud** (a slow, smooth pad), **Vowel Drift** (a vowel that moves as
you hold a note) and **Glass Swarm** (small, quick, wide grains).

## Controls

- **Position:** where in the built-in sound the grains start (0 to 1).
- **Scan:** how many seconds the grains take to move across the whole sound
  (0.1 to 8). Short is a fast shimmer; long is a slow change.
- **Texture:** low is large, sparse grains near the middle; high is small,
  dense grains spread wide across left and right with a little detune.
- **Release:** the fade after you let go (0.02 to 2 seconds).

Notes fade in gently and keep sounding while held (or while the sustain
pedal is down). Up to 8 notes play at once; a ninth takes over the oldest.

Changing the starting sound clears held notes and replaces the four
controls. Edits save with the project, and switching to another instrument
and back keeps them.

## Mixer, MIDI and export

The SYNTH strip controls volume, pan, mute, solo, sends and processing.
Master pitch, velocity, the sustain pedal and a +/-2-semitone pitch wheel
work as they do for the other built-in voices. The **SYNTH part's WAV
export** renders this voice with its edited controls.
